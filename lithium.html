POWER SYSTEMS

Lithium power, wired with care.

Converting a golf cart or bass boat to lithium, or building any battery project, depends on the right pack and charger, correct cable sizing, protected circuits, and secure terminals. Plan the system before making the first connection.

Build notes

Fuse close to the source, crimp with the right die, cover exposed conductors, and verify polarity with a meter before energizing.

Practical pick

Label every cable and leave service loops so maintenance is straightforward.
