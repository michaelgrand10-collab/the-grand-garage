What's NOT on Amazon

Buy these locally, or check with your town first:

  • Candy and treats: buy them locally and check whether your parade lets you throw candy or only hand it out.
  • A full charge for the cart and any service it needs: see your local golf cart shop.
  • Parade registration and community rules: ask your HOA or the event organizer.

Project overview

  1. Check the parade rules. Read the organizer's rules on lights, music volume, candy (handing out vs. throwing), lineup time and who may drive. Pick your theme.
  2. Prep the cart. Wash the cart, fully charge it and check the headlights, brakes and horn.
  3. Load and test the lights. Put AA batteries in the string-light boxes and test every mode the night before. Kit items: Orange and Purple Battery String Lights, 2-Pack (100 LED, 35.6 ft); Amazon Basics AA Alkaline Batteries, 20-Pack.
  4. Attach the decorations. Zip-tie garlands, banners, webs and props to the roof struts, rails and seat back, and snip the tails flush. Keep everything clear of the wheels, pedals, steering and headlights. Kit items: Suncalla 25-Piece Halloween Golf Cart Decoration Kit; Tryly Witch-Theme Halloween Golf Cart Decoration Kit; 12 in. Heavy-Duty UV-Resistant Zip Ties, 100-Pack; Klein Tools D275-5 5 in. Flush-Cutting Pliers; EFFICERE 24-Piece Bungee Cord Assortment.
  5. Run the string lights. Wrap the lights along the roof struts and zip-tie the battery boxes where you can reach the switch. Kit items: Orange and Purple Battery String Lights, 2-Pack (100 LED, 35.6 ft); 12 in. Heavy-Duty UV-Resistant Zip Ties, 100-Pack; Klein Tools D275-5 5 in. Flush-Cutting Pliers.
  6. Install the underglow (Pro). With the key off, install the underglow kit to the cart battery following the kit's instructions, and route the wires away from moving parts. Kit items: 12 in. Heavy-Duty UV-Resistant Zip Ties, 100-Pack; Roykaw Golf Cart Underglow LED Light Kit (app and remote).
  7. Mount the music (Better, Pro). Strap the speaker mount to a rail, seat the speaker and pair your phone. Kit items: Anker Soundcore 2 Portable Bluetooth Speaker; JBL Flip 6 Portable Waterproof Speaker; HomeMount Golf Cart Speaker Strap Mount.
  8. Load the candy and glow sticks (Better, Pro). Fill the LED pumpkin buckets and hand out glow sticks to riders and kids along the route. Kit items: JOYIN LED Light-Up Pumpkin Candy Buckets, 2-Pack; PartySticks 8 in. Glow Sticks, 400-Pack.
  9. Do a dusk test drive. Drive a slow lap at dusk, tug-test every piece, re-tie anything loose and make sure nothing blocks the driver's view.

Safety: decorations must never block the driver's view, lights, mirrors, pedals or steering. Keep everything clear of the wheels, drive slowly, keep riders seated, and follow your community's age and licensing rules for drivers.

This is a general overview, not a substitute for the manufacturer's instructions, a full set of plans, or your local codes and permits.
