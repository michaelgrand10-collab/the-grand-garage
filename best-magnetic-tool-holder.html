FAQ

Will a magnetic bar hold heavy tools?

Good bars hold wrenches, pliers and hammers. Mount into studs, not just drywall anchors, for anything over a few pounds.

Will magnets magnetize my screwdrivers?

Slightly, and it's usually a plus: tips pick up screws more easily.

Can I use one for kitchen knives?

Some double-sided bars are sold for knives too, but keep shop and kitchen bars separate.

How high should I mount it?

Eye level above the bench, about 50 to 60 inches from the floor, so tools are easy to see and grab.
