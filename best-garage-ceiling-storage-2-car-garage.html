FAQ

How much ceiling storage fits in a 2-car garage?

Most 2-car garages fit two 4x8 racks, one over each bay, plus a smaller 3x6 or tote rails over the door track or workbench. Check your door opener and track clearance first.

Do I need to hit ceiling joists?

Yes. Overhead racks are rated for wood joists. Locate each joist center with a stud finder or a small test nail, and never hang a rack from drywall alone.

What drop height should I pick?

Measure from the ceiling to the top of your tallest vehicle with the hatch or tailgate open, then subtract a few inches of safety margin.

Is a rack or tote rails better?

Racks hold anything, including odd shapes. Rails are faster if everything you store is already in matching totes.
