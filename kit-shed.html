What's NOT on Amazon

Buy these locally, or check with your town first:

  • Lumber: pressure-treated 4x4 or 4x6 skids, pressure-treated 2x6 floor joists, 2x4 studs and plates, 2x6 rafters and ridge board, 1x trim boards
  • Sheet goods: 3/4 in. tongue-and-groove plywood (floor), 1/2 in. OSB or plywood (roof), T1-11 siding panels
  • Roofing: drip edge, roofing underlayment, shingles, ridge cap, 1-1/4 in. roofing nails and cap nails
  • Siding and trim nails (galvanized, sized for your siding), exterior caulk, primer and paint or stain
  • Gravel or concrete blocks for the foundation; a shovel, rake and wheelbarrow if you don't have them
  • Permits and inspections: check with your local building department first, and call 811 before digging

Project overview

  1. Plan, permit and lay out. Check zoning setbacks, HOA rules and whether your town needs a permit for an 80 sq ft shed. Call 811 before you dig or drive anchors. Measure and mark the footprint.
