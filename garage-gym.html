TRAIN AT HOME

A space-saving garage gym.

The best garage gym leaves room for the car and the project. Start with adjustable dumbbells, a folding bench, resistance bands, and a durable mat.

Plan the footprint

  • Measure the floor and door swing before choosing storage.
  • Measure the ceiling before installing racks or pull-up hardware.
  • Keep a clear landing area and protect concrete with a proper mat.
  • Store bands and dumbbells where they are visible and easy to return.
