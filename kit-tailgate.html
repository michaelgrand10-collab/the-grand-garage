What's NOT on Amazon

Buy these locally, or check with your town first:

  • Propane: 1 lb cylinders (Good, Better) or a 20 lb tank (Pro) from a local hardware store or exchange.
  • Ice, food, drinks, paper plates, foil and paper towels from your local grocery
  • Sand (or gravel) to fill the canopy weight bags from a local hardware store
  • Parking passes and lot rules from your venue

Project overview

  1. Check the lot rules. Check the stadium or venue's rules on canopies, open flames and propane, glass and lot hours, and plan the menu.
  2. Pack the night before. Load the cooler with ice and food (raw meat sealed and low), and pack the griddle, tools, propane and table. Kit items: Coleman Classic 62 Qt Wheeled Cooler; YETI Roadie 48 Wheeled Cooler.
  3. Pitch the shade (Better, Pro). Pop up the canopy and strap a filled sand bag to every leg before the wind finds it. Kit items: 10x10 Pop-Up Canopy with One-Push Center Lock; ABCCANOPY Canopy Weight Bags, 4-Pack (no sand).
  4. Set up camp. Unfold the table and chairs and set out the trash bag, first-aid kit and fire extinguisher where everyone can see them. Kit items: Coleman Portable Camping Chair with 4-Can Cooler; 4 ft Fold-in-Half Portable Utility Table; Amazon Basics 42-Gallon 3 Mil Contractor Bags, 50 Count; Ougist 2.5 lb ABC Fire Extinguisher (1-A:10-B:C); Swiss Safe 200-Piece First Aid Kit.
  5. Fire up the griddle. Set the griddle on the table outside the canopy, connect the propane and preheat it. Kit items: Blackstone 1666 22 in. Tabletop Griddle; Blackstone 1813 22 in. Tabletop Griddle with Hood; 4 ft Fold-in-Half Portable Utility Table; Blackstone 5471 Propane Adapter Hose and Regulator for 20 lb Tank; Ougist 2.5 lb ABC Fire Extinguisher (1-A:10-B:C).
  6. Cook. Cook with the spatulas and scraper, and check doneness with the thermometer. Kit items: Blackstone 1666 22 in. Tabletop Griddle; Blackstone 1813 22 in. Tabletop Griddle with Hood; Blackstone Essential Griddle Accessory Toolkit, 6-Piece; TempPro TempSwift Instant-Read Meat Thermometer; MEATER Plus Wireless Smart Meat Thermometer.
  7. Play (Better, Pro). Set up cornhole about 27 ft apart, front edge to front edge, and start the music. Kit items: GoSports 4x2 ft Regulation Cornhole Set (8 bags); JBL Charge 6 Portable Bluetooth Speaker.
  8. Clean up. Turn off the propane, let the griddle cool, scrape it, bag all the trash and leave the spot cleaner than you found it. Kit items: Blackstone Essential Griddle Accessory Toolkit, 6-Piece; Amazon Basics 42-Gallon 3 Mil Contractor Bags, 50 Count.

Safety: cook only outside the canopy, away from vehicles and fabric, and never in an enclosed space. Keep the extinguisher within reach, turn off the propane when you're done, follow venue rules, and keep cold food cold.

This is a general overview, not a substitute for the manufacturer's instructions, a full set of plans, or your local codes and permits.
