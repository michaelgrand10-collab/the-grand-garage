What's NOT on Amazon

Pick these up locally:

  • Ice, drinks and snacks from your grocery store
  • Sunscreen and bug spray from your pharmacy
  • Sand or gravel to fill the canopy weight bags, from your hardware store
  • Uniforms, balls, shin guards and other team gear from your team or league
  • Field rules, schedules and parking info from your league

How to pack the trunk

  1. Organizer first. Set the trunk organizer against the back seat and fill it with the small stuff that stays in the car all season: the first aid kit, the power bank, wipes and spare socks. Kit items: trunk organizer, first aid kit, power bank.
  2. Flat and folding gear next. Lay the folded wagon flat on the floor, then the chairs, the canopy, the blanket and the ball bag on top. Heavy things go low and toward the seats. Kit items: wagon, chairs, canopy, blanket, ball bag, speaker.
  3. Cooler in last. Load the cooler at the tailgate so it's the first thing out. Pack drinks and snacks the night before, and fill the tumbler on your way out. Kit items: wheeled cooler, tumbler.
  4. One trip to the field. Unfold the wagon, load the cooler, chairs, blanket and ball bag, and roll it all to the sideline. Kit items: wagon.
  5. Set up shade. Pop up the canopy, then strap a filled weight bag to every leg before the wind finds it. Kit items: canopy, canopy weights.
  6. Reset for next week. Drain the cooler, air out the mesh bag, recharge the power bank and speaker, and restock the first aid kit.

Safety: the wagon is for gear only, so no riders. Weight every canopy leg and take the canopy down if the wind picks up. Keep the first aid kit where any adult can find it, and don't leave the power bank or speaker baking on the dashboard. Follow your field's rules on canopies, speakers and wagons on turf.

This is a general packing guide. Follow each manufacturer's instructions and your league's rules.
