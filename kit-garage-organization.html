What's NOT on Amazon

Buy these locally, or check with your town first:

  • Donation drop-off and bulk-trash pickup: check your local hauler or town schedule.
  • Hazardous waste (old paint, fuel, chemicals): take it to your county's household hazardous-waste drop-off and don't bag it with the trash.
  • A bucket and hose water for the floor scrub, which you likely already have

Project overview

  1. Empty and sort. Pull everything out onto the driveway, sort it into keep, donate and trash, and bag the trash as you go. Kit items: Amazon Basics 42-Gallon 3 Mil Contractor Bags, 50 Count; Wells Lamont Cowhide Leather Work Gloves.
  2. Clean the floor. Sweep, then scrub oil and grime with diluted degreaser and the push broom. Rinse and let it dry. Kit items: Simple Green Industrial Cleaner and Degreaser; CLEANHOME 24 in. Push Broom; Wells Lamont Cowhide Leather Work Gloves.
  3. Plan the zones. Measure the walls and mark zones for shelving, long-handled tools, bikes and seasonal bins. Keep a clear path to the car doors. Kit items: Komelon SL2825 25 ft Self-Lock Tape Measure.
  4. Build the shelving. Assemble the 5-shelf unit, which needs no tools, and level its feet. Kit items: Amazon Basics 5-Shelf Heavy-Duty Steel Shelving, 36 x 14 x 72 in..
  5. Find the studs and mount the wall storage. Locate and mark the studs. Mount the hooks into studs (Good), or level and screw the FastTrack rails into every stud they cross, with anchors between studs as the instructions specify (Better, Pro). Kit items: Komelon SL2825 25 ft Self-Lock Tape Measure; Franklin Sensors ProSensor M150 Stud Finder; Kapro 770 Exodus 48 in. Level; KOFANI Heavy-Duty Steel Garage Hooks, 16-Pack; Rubbermaid FastTrack Garage Wall System, 24-Piece Kit (6 rails, 18 hooks); Wells Lamont Cowhide Leather Work Gloves; 3M Virtua CCS Anti-Fog Safety Glasses (ANSI Z87).
  6. Install the ceiling rack (Pro). Locate the ceiling joists and lag-bolt the overhead rack to them per FLEXIMOUNTS' instructions. Use a helper and a stable ladder. Kit items: Franklin Sensors ProSensor M150 Stud Finder; FLEXIMOUNTS 4x8 Overhead Garage Ceiling Storage Rack with 6 Hooks; Wells Lamont Cowhide Leather Work Gloves; 3M Virtua CCS Anti-Fog Safety Glasses (ANSI Z87).
  7. Bin and label. Pack small items into the latching bins, label each one and shelve them by zone. Kit items: Brother P-touch PT-M95 Label Maker Bundle (4 tapes); Amazon Basics 5-Shelf Heavy-Duty Steel Shelving, 36 x 14 x 72 in.; Amazon Basics 12 Qt Clear Latching Storage Bins, 6-Pack.
  8. Hang it all up. Hang ladders, cords, yard tools and bikes, and keep heavy items low. Kit items: KOFANI Heavy-Duty Steel Garage Hooks, 16-Pack; Rubbermaid FastTrack Garage Wall System, 24-Piece Kit (6 rails, 18 hooks); Wells Lamont Cowhide Leather Work Gloves.

Safety: wear gloves and eye protection when drilling, especially overhead. Mount hooks and racks only into wood studs or joists as each manufacturer specifies, stay within the rated loads, and use a helper for anything overhead. Never store fuel or chemicals overhead.

This is a general overview, not a substitute for the manufacturer's instructions, a full set of plans, or your local codes and permits.
