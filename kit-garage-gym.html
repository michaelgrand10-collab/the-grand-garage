What's NOT on Amazon

Buy these locally, or check with your town first:

  • Thick rubber stall mats (3/4 in.) for heavy barbell work. They're heavy to ship, so look for them at a local farm-supply store.
  • Any wall repair or extra blocking if your studs aren't where the rack needs them; check your garage framing first.
  • Good lighting and a fan for the training area if your garage needs them

Project overview

  1. Measure the space. Measure the floor footprint and ceiling height. Leave room to walk out of the rack and load plates on both ends of a 7 ft bar, and check for garage-door tracks and lights overhead. Kit items: Komelon SL2825 25 ft Self-Lock Tape Measure.
  2. Clean and lay the floor. Sweep the slab and snap the interlocking mats together where you'll train. Kit items: ProsourceFit 1/2 in. Puzzle Exercise Mat, 24 sq ft (6 tiles).
  3. Find the studs (Better, Pro). Use the stud finder to locate and mark two wood studs where the rack will mount. Kit items: Franklin Sensors ProSensor M150 Stud Finder.
  4. Mount the folding rack (Better, Pro). Follow Mikolo's instructions to mount the rack to the studs with the included hardware, then check that it folds and locks. Kit items: Komelon SL2825 25 ft Self-Lock Tape Measure; Mikolo Folding Wall-Mounted Squat Rack with Safety Catches (2x2, 1,000 lb).
  5. Set the J-hooks and safety catches (Better, Pro). Set the J-hooks and safety catches to your height before you load the bar. Kit items: Mikolo Folding Wall-Mounted Squat Rack with Safety Catches (2x2, 1,000 lb).
  6. Assemble the bench. Build the bench and check every adjustment pin. Kit items: FLYBIRD WB5 Adjustable Weight Bench.
  7. Set up the weights. Load the barbell evenly and lock the plates with collars, or set the dumbbells and kettlebell on the floor beside the bench. Kit items: CAP Barbell 44 lb Olympic Bar, 7 ft; CAP 160 lb Economy Olympic Bumper Plate Set (pairs of 10, 25, 45); Olympic 2 in. Quick-Release Barbell Collars, Pair; FLYBIRD WB5 Adjustable Weight Bench; PowerBlock Elite EXP Adjustable Dumbbells, Stage 1 (5 to 50 lb pair); BowFlex SelectTech 840 Adjustable Kettlebell (8 to 40 lb); Fit Simplify Resistance Loop Bands, Set of 5.
  8. Store it and check it. Hang the bands and keep a clear lane. Before each session, check the rack bolts, pins and collars. Kit items: Olympic 2 in. Quick-Release Barbell Collars, Pair; Fit Simplify Resistance Loop Bands, Set of 5.

Safety: stay within every manufacturer's weight ratings, use collars and the rack's safety catches, mount racks only to solid wood studs as the instructions specify, and keep kids and pets out of the lifting lane. If you're new to lifting, learn the movements from a qualified coach.
