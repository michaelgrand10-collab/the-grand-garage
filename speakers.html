Wiring layout

  • Battery positive runs to the master breaker or inline fuse, mounted within a few inches of the battery.
  • From the breaker, go through the waterproof power rocker switch to the amp's positive input.
  • Battery negative runs to the amp's negative input. The accessories share that common ground.
  • The amp's speaker output goes to the 8" speaker. The passive radiators just mount in the case and aren't wired.
  • The charging port goes to the battery through its own inline fuse. Charge only with a charger that matches the battery chemistry and voltage (14.6V for 12V LiFePO4, 29.2V for 24V LiFePO4).
  • The USB-C PD outlet, battery display, fan (through the thermostat), and LED ring all tap in after the breaker, each on its own small fuse or switch.
  • DSP starting points: high-pass at 60 Hz, low-pass at 120 Hz only on a dedicated bass channel, a bass boost of +4 to +6 dB around 45-60 Hz, and a limiter at -1 dBFS. Tune by ear at moderate volume, then check for distortion at max.

Quality tips

  • Use marine-grade speakers.
  • Use stainless bolts with washers and nylon lock nuts.
  • Put the fuse close to the battery.
  • Heat-shrink every connection.
  • Strap the battery down so it can't shift.
  • Line the lid and walls with sound deadener.
  • Seal every cutout with silicone.
  • Finish it with a custom front spacer ring and grill.

Premium upgrades

  • Battery % meter
  • USB-C PD charging output
  • Rubber feet
  • Internal addressable LED ring
  • Quick-disconnect speaker plug
  • Waterproof charging-port cap

Safety first: lithium batteries need a proper BMS, a fuse near the battery, and a matched charger. Don't leave a pack charging unattended.
