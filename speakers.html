LISTENING ROOM

DIY speakers that fit the room.

A good speaker project starts with the listening space, not a flashy driver. Sketch the cabinet, account for bracing and damping, and keep the crossover accessible while you learn.

Build notes

Use a square, seal every joint, and label polarity before closing the cabinet. Sanding and finish work usually take longer than the cut list.

Practical pick

Budget for clamps, terminal cups, wire, and measurement time.
