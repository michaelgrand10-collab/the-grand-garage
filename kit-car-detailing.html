Project overview

  1. Set up in the shade. Park in the shade with the paint cool. Fill three buckets: wheels, soapy wash with a grit guard, and clean rinse with a grit guard. Kit items: United Solutions 5-Gallon Bucket, 3-Pack; VIKING Car Wash Grit Trap Bucket Insert, 2-Pack.
  2. Wheels and tires first. Spray the wheel and tire cleaner on one wheel at a time, scrub with the wheel brush, and rinse. Kit items: United Solutions 5-Gallon Bucket, 3-Pack; Armor All Extreme Wheel and Tire Cleaner, 24 oz; Mothers Wheel and Fender Brush, 10 in.; Inspire Black Nitrile Gloves, Heavy Duty 6 Mil; 3M Virtua CCS Anti-Fog Safety Glasses (ANSI Z87).
  3. Pre-rinse or foam. Rinse loose dirt off the car top to bottom. In Pro, cover it with foam from the cannon, let it dwell and rinse. Kit items: Meguiar's Gold Class Car Wash, 1 Gallon; Westinghouse ePX3500 Electric Pressure Washer (2500 max PSI); AstroAI Foam Cannon, 1/4 in. Quick Connect, 1 L; 3M Virtua CCS Anti-Fog Safety Glasses (ANSI Z87).
  4. Two-bucket wash. Wash one panel at a time from the roof down with a soapy mitt, rinsing the mitt against the grit guard before every reload. Kit items: United Solutions 5-Gallon Bucket, 3-Pack; VIKING Car Wash Grit Trap Bucket Insert, 2-Pack; Meguiar's Gold Class Car Wash, 1 Gallon; Chemical Guys Chenille Microfiber Wash Mitt, 2-Pack.
  5. Rinse and dry. Give it a final rinse and blot-dry with the big drying towel. Kit items: ExoForma Mega Car Drying Towel (1200 GSM).
  6. Decontaminate (Better, Pro). Spray the iron remover on the paint and wheels and rinse, then clay each panel with plenty of lube until it feels glass-smooth. Kit items: Turtle Wax Hybrid Solutions Iron Remover and Wheel Cleaner, 23 oz; Meguiar's Smooth Surface Clay Kit (3 bars, Quik Detailer, towel); Inspire Black Nitrile Gloves, Heavy Duty 6 Mil; 3M Virtua CCS Anti-Fog Safety Glasses (ANSI Z87).
  7. Protect. Mist the ceramic spray wax onto one panel at a time and buff it off with clean microfiber towels. Kit items: Turtle Wax Hybrid Solutions Ceramic Spray Wax Coating, 16 oz; USANOOKS Microfiber Cleaning Cloths, 12-Pack.
  8. Glass and tires. Clean the glass inside and out, then foam the tires for a dark finish. Kit items: USANOOKS Microfiber Cleaning Cloths, 12-Pack; Invisible Glass Auto Glass Cleaner, 22 oz; Meguiar's Hot Shine Tire Foam.
  9. Interior. Vacuum the seats, carpets and crevices (Better, Pro), then wipe the dash, doors and vinyl with interior cleaner. Kit items: USANOOKS Microfiber Cleaning Cloths, 12-Pack; Chemical Guys Total Interior Cleaner and Protectant, 16 oz; BLACK+DECKER dustbuster AdvancedClean Cordless Handheld Vacuum.
  10. Headlights (Pro). Restore hazy headlights with the ceramic wipe kit. Kit items: CERAKOTE Ceramic Headlight Restoration Kit.

Safety: wear nitrile gloves and eye protection with wheel cleaners and iron remover, and work in a ventilated area. Never point a pressure washer at people, pets or tire sidewalls, and keep its electrical connections dry. Read every product label before you use it.
