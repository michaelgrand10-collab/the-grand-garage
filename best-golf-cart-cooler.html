FAQ

Will a mounted cooler fit my cart?

Bracket kits are model-specific. Check your cart make, model and year against the listing before ordering.

Soft or mounted?

Soft coolers work on any cart and come inside with you. Mounted coolers stay put and free up seat space.

Are coolers allowed on golf courses?

Most courses allow coolers, but some ban outside alcohol. Check your club's rules.

How do I keep drinks cold longer?

Pre-chill the cooler and drinks, use ice packs under loose ice, and park it in the shade.
