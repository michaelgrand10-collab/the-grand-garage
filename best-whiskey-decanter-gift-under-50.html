FAQ

Is crystal or glass better for a decanter?

For a gift, lead-free glass is the safe pick. It looks the part and is fine for storing spirits long term.

How long can whiskey stay in a decanter?

Months, if the stopper seals well. Whiskey doesn't age in glass, but a big air gap slowly flattens the aroma, so keep it at least half full.

Do these come gift-wrapped?

Most of these ship in a presentation box. Amazon gift wrap availability varies by listing.

Can I get one engraved?

Engraved sets cost more than $50, so none are on this list. A gift-boxed set plus a handwritten card lands just as well.
