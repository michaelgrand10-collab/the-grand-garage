Build steps

  • Lay the monitor face-down on the lid foam, trace it, and cut the foam so the screen sits flat against the lid shell.
  • Mark the monitor's VESA holes on the lid, then drill through the shell. Mount the monitor with machine screws that match its holes (M4 on most small monitors), with a fender washer on the outside of the case under each screw head so the screws can't pull through. Pick a length that reaches the full thread depth without bottoming out. A dab of silicone under each washer keeps water out.
  • If you prefer the backer-plate method from my first sheet, bolt the aluminum plate and VESA adapter inside the lid first, then mount the monitor to the adapter.
  • Cut the base foam for the console, the power strip, and two controllers. Leave an air channel around the console's vents.
  • Mount the two USB fans to pull hot air out of the base, and cut matching vent openings. Never run the console with the case closed.
  • Install the AC inlet in the case wall, run the short extension cord to the power strip, and route the angled HDMI from the console to the monitor. Add the panel-mount HDMI and USB ports if you want clean side access.
  • Tie down every cable so nothing snags when the lid closes.

Quality tips

  • Measure the lid interior and the monitor with the stand removed before you buy anything.
  • Use stainless screws and fender washers, and seal every hole you drill.
  • Keep the console flat and give it breathing room; heat is the enemy in a closed box.
  • Strap the power strip and cables down so they don't rattle in transport.
  • Pack the controllers and cords in their own foam pockets.

Safety first: the AC inlet carries 120V household power. Wire it with the correct gauge, polarity, and ground, keep every connection enclosed, and never make or use a cord with a plug on both ends. If you're not comfortable wiring 120V, have a qualified person do it, or skip the inlet and pass the power strip's cord out through a grommeted notch instead. Unplug everything before you close the case.
