Woodworking that gets used.

Useful shop furniture rewards accuracy more than speed. Break down sheet goods with a straightedge, dry-fit before glue, and give your finish enough cure time.

A calmer workflow

  • Keep a sharp marking knife and a reliable square close by.
  • Make a cut diagram before turning on the saw.
  • Store small offcuts by thickness so they become future jigs.
